Built around the barbell, not the treadmill.

Most gyms are built for the average member. FORGE is built for the member who wants to get stronger every single week and can tell the difference between a good set and a wasted one. We keep the floor uncluttered, the plates loaded, and the coaching direct.

Every new member starts with a form assessment, not a sales pitch. You'll know your working weights before you know where the water cooler is.
